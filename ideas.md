# Chosen Direction — Thermal Cartography

## Design Movement

**Thermal Cartography** blends contemporary editorial design with scientific field notation and satellite-imagery abstraction. The reference's cinematic composition and smooth movement are interpreted through original data-led landscapes rather than its imagery, content, or brand language.

## Core Principles

1. **Landscape, not dashboard:** Environmental intelligence appears as living terrain, maps, signal paths, and editorial data marks rather than rows of generic product cards.
2. **Selective heat:** Most of the site remains quiet and mineral-like so orange and red carry immediate semantic weight whenever risk, activity, or change is present.
3. **Directed movement:** Motion is tied to scanning, signal propagation, elevation, and intervention; decorative fade-ins are avoided.
4. **Humanly legible systems:** Dense intelligence is summarized in large typographic moments, clear labels, and generous empty space.

## Color Philosophy

The site begins in **mineral paper** (#F2F0E9): calm enough for serious enterprise use and intentionally warm enough to signal climate rather than generic software. **Basalt** (#151515) grounds typography and maps. **Solar orange** (#FF6B2C) is the ownable active signal; it appears in hotspots, decision states, and decisive calls to action. Amber carries observing states, deep red denotes critical conditions, and muted sage is reserved for verified/safe outcomes. Dark basalt sections create dramatic chapters, rather than an always-dark interface.

## Layout Paradigm

The page is structured as a **climate transect**: an oversized editorial hero descends into a horizontal cross-section of intelligence layers, then an operational field report, and finally a dark, decisive deployment close. Sections use generous horizontal offsets, full-bleed visual fields, pinned/sticky moments, and asymmetric data anchoring instead of centered card grids.

## Signature Elements

1. **Topographic scanlines:** Fine contour-like lines and coordinate ticks create a precise environmental context throughout the site.
2. **Heat blooms:** Soft, organic orange-to-red fields indicate live thermal activity; they animate slowly like radiating temperature.
3. **Field labels:** Compact monospaced labels, coordinates, and markers provide an intelligence-report vocabulary alongside large human-centered headings.

## Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should feel like interrogating a field instrument. Buttons move toward the cursor on desktop, hotspots update a single information panel, cards subtly advance in physical space, and accordion lines open with the calm precision of a report being expanded. Keyboard and reduced-motion pathways remain direct and complete.

## Animation

The entry sequence is brief: the HEATCHECK wordmark resolves in individual letter groups, then the hero's terrain settles into view. Scrolling shifts foreground typography at a different rate from the map, brings scanlines into focus, and turns the Observe → Analyze → Decide → Act → Verify story into a pinned visual sequence. Heat blooms use low-amplitude scale and opacity pulses. The default easing is `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` with transform and opacity favored for performance. Reduced-motion users receive complete visual states without motion.

## Typography System

**Manrope** provides the large display voice: dense, contemporary, and stable at the hero scale. **DM Mono** provides field labels, metrics, timestamps, and system states. Headlines are tight and generously scaled; body text is measured and left-aligned. Section labels use uppercase mono lettering and a small amber square, while operational numbers use tabular mono figures where possible.

## Brand Essence

**Heatcheck is the autonomous operating system for teams that need to see heat risk early and change work before exposure becomes harm.**

Personality: **measured, vigilant, incisive.**

## Brand Voice

The voice is compact, declarative, and operational. It avoids hype in favor of a precise statement followed by a clear implication. Headlines make the condition and response unmistakable; CTAs describe the next operational move.

Example lines: “Heat doesn't wait. Neither should your schedule.” and “See the signal. Change the outcome.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The logo is a **four-corner thermal mark**: a square field with a concentric offset core that evokes both a heat hotspot and a coordinate target. It is used as a solid basalt mark on light surfaces and a warm-white mark on dark surfaces. The accompanying wordmark uses a custom-spaced Manrope treatment with the crossbar concept echoed in the “A”.

## Signature Brand Color

**Solar Signal Orange — #FF6B2C.**

## Style Decisions

- Every major section carries at least one **cartographic intelligence cue**: a coordinate, scanline field, indexed marker, terrain slice, heat bloom, or report seal. Commercial sections are treated as deployment materials rather than generic product packaging.
- The four-corner thermal mark recurs as brand identity and an **instrumental field marker** across the navigation, commercial deployment area, field notes, dashboard, maps, and report-style footer.
- Commercial language stays operational: **field signal, response layer, deployment, condition, intervention, readiness, and governance** replace generic SaaS packaging language where context allows.

# Cinematic Thermal Motion Plan

Heatcheck will use a more energetic **Thermal Surge** system. It will retain the current mineral-to-basalt chapters but introduce stronger, purposeful scene changes: a directional thermal wash at the hero, a scanning transect through the operating loop, an active observatory field in the Intelligence chapter, terrain flare transitions on response imagery, and a glowing network pulse within the closing CTA.

The motion is designed as an operational readout rather than decorative animation. A cursor-responsive thermal halo will react only on fine-pointer devices. The operating loop will read as a moving transect, with signal travel triggered by hover or focus. The Intelligence chapter will gain continuously moving contour lines, orbiting location markers, and staged card illumination. The response image will carry an incident scan sweep and a live severity seal.

All major animation is constrained to `transform`, `opacity`, and composited background effects. Timing escalates through the page: 500–900 milliseconds for section scenes, 180–300 milliseconds for interaction feedback, and 2–7 seconds for ambient signals. Reduced-motion settings will remove travel and hover-dependent movement while keeping the active reading, field labels, and visual hierarchy intact.

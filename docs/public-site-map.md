# Heatcheck Public Site Map

## Home Page

The home page will retain only the **hero**, the short **Observe → Analyze → Decide → Act → Verify** operating loop, a compact **Intelligence** preview, one illustrative response story, a concise solutions preview, and the primary call to action. This preserves the strongest product signals while preventing the landing page from becoming a complete product manual.

## Linked Detail Pages

| Route | Purpose | Primary content |
|---|---|---|
| `/platform` | Explain the end-to-end product operating model. | Monitoring, decisioning, response, verification, and dashboard overview. |
| `/intelligence` | Present every Intelligence layer as a full, readable field module. | Thermal, environmental, AI reasoning, autonomous response, and urban heat intelligence. |
| `/solutions` | Show how Heatcheck applies to operational contexts. | Logistics, construction, warehousing, campuses, municipalities, and manufacturing. |
| `/company` | Provide deployment guidance, governance context, plans, FAQ, and contact paths. | Deployment levels, governance, answers, and a contact call to action. |

## Navigation Principle

The public navigation will link to pages rather than long in-page anchors. The home-page Intelligence preview will sit immediately after the operating loop and before the response story, with a single explicit link to `/intelligence`. On mobile, all five Intelligence preview cards will display as stacked, full-width cards rather than a clipped horizontal rail.
